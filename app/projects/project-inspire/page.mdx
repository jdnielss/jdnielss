# Full Stack Software Engineer — INSPIRE JEC

<Cover src="/assets/images/inspire.png" alt="inspire.cdn-jec.co.id" caption="inspire.cdn-jec.co.id" />

Institut Pendidikan dan Riset JEC (INSPIRE JEC) is an Indonesia Non-Profit Organization under Yayasan Pilar Mitra Pendidikan based at JEC Eye Hospitals and Clinics.

Our scope encompasses education, research, community service, and professional development, integrated with the ophthalmological healthcare activities of the JEC Group, which operated eye hospitals and clinics throughout Indonesia.

JEC is nationally and internationally acclaimed modern eye hospital in Indonesia with state-of-the-art equipment. INSPIRE JEC is a highly sought-after institution for practicing ophthalmologist and future ophthalmologist seeking to advance their clinical expertise and professional competence.

- **SEO & GEO Optimization**: Implemented strategies to improve search engine visibility and location-based discovery.
- **Custom Payload CMS**: Developed a tailored content management system to streamline content updates and management.
